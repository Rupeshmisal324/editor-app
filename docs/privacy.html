Privacy Policy – Editor

Effective date: 20 November 2026

Editor (“the app”) is a photo and video editor for iPhone and Android, published by [DEVELOPER NAME] (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have.

In short: your photos and videos are edited on your device and are never uploaded to us. We do not ask you to create an account. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, which uses device information to show and measure ads, and you can buy “Remove ads” through the App Store or Google Play.

1. Your photos and videos

Photos and videos you choose, take with the camera, or record are processed only on your device. Your edits are kept in the app's own storage (the Library) until you delete them, clear the Library in Settings, or uninstall the app. When you tap Save, a copy goes to your device's photo library; when you tap Share, the file goes only to the app or person you pick. We never receive your photos or videos.

2. Permissions the app asks for

PermissionWhy
Photos / mediaTo open the photos and videos you pick and to save your edits to your photo library.
CameraTo take a photo or record a video to edit.
MicrophoneTo record sound with videos and to record voice-overs in the video editor.
Tracking (iPhone only)Only if you allow it: lets ads be more relevant to you (see section 4).

You can change these permissions at any time in your device settings. The app does not use your location.

3. Music search

When you search for music in the video editor, your search words are sent to Apple's public iTunes Search service, and song previews and artwork are loaded from Apple's servers. Apple receives your search words and your device's IP address, as it does for any web request. This is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy. We do not store your searches.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads from Google AdMob, unless you buy “Remove ads”. To show, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA, the latter only if you allow tracking),
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • ad interactions (ads shown and tapped) and diagnostic information.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent form. You can change your choice any time in Settings → Privacy options. On iPhone you can stop tracking in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; on Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

5. Purchases (“Remove ads”)

Payments are handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play); we never see your card or payment details. To check whether you have bought “Remove ads” and to restore it on a new device, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives a random app user ID, your purchase receipt (product, price, date, country) and basic device information. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

6. What we do not do

  • We do not have user accounts and do not collect your name, email or contacts.
  • We do not upload, view or sell your photos and videos.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

7. Keeping and deleting information

Your edits stay on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app. Information held by Google (ads) and RevenueCat (purchases) is kept according to their own policies. To ask us to delete purchase records linked to your app user ID, contact us (section 10).

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, to object to or limit its use, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold your photos, videos or an account, most requests are best handled through the settings above or with Google and RevenueCat directly; we will help with any request sent to us.

9. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
